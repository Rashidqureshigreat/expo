# 🚀 Welcome to VisualDream Job Portal

VisualDream is your trusted partner for finding **real online jobs** and **freelance opportunities** — all in one place.  
Follow the steps below to get started and start earning from home today 💼

---

<Step label="1">
## Create your account
Visit [VisualDream Job Portal](https://visualdream.in) and sign up for a free account.  
Use your valid email and mobile number for verification.

<ContentSpotlight 
  alt="VisualDream signup page"
  src="/images/signup.png"
  className="max-w-[540px]"
/>

</Step>

---

<Step label="2">
## Complete your profile

Add your skills, education, and interests. This helps employers and affiliate programs find you faster.

✅ Tip: The more detailed your profile, the more job matches you’ll receive.

</Step>

---

<Step label="3">
## Browse and apply for jobs

Once your profile is complete, head to the **Jobs** section and explore categories:
- Work From Home 💻  
- Data Entry 🧾  
- Affiliate Promotion 🌐  
- Freelancing ✍️

Click **Apply** on any job to start your journey.

<Collapsible summary="Having trouble applying?">
Make sure:
- You are logged in
- Your profile is at least 80% complete
- The job is still active (not expired)
</Collapsible>

</Step>

---

<Step label="4">
## Start earning and tracking progress

You can track:
- Applied jobs  
- Referral earnings  
- Weekly rewards  

Go to **Dashboard → Earnings** to monitor your income.

<Terminal cmd={['Dashboard > Earnings']} />

</Step>

---

## 💡 Tips for Success

- Join our Telegram or WhatsApp channel for job alerts.
- Share your referral link to earn bonus rewards.

<Collapsible summary="Changes not showing up on your device?">

Expo Go is configured by default to automatically reload the app whenever a file is changed, but let's make sure to go over the steps to enable it in case somehow things aren't working.

- Make sure you have the [development mode enabled in Expo CLI](/workflow/development-mode#development-mode).
- Close the Expo app and reopen it.
- Once the app is open again, shake your device to reveal the developer menu. If you are using an emulator, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> for Android or <kbd>Cmd ⌘</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> for iOS.
- If you see **Enable Fast Refresh**, press it. If you see **Disable Fast Refresh**, dismiss the developer menu. Now try making another change.
